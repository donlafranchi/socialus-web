Events

Fox & Goose is an iconic pub and music venue that has been celebrating the creativity of Sacramento’s vibrant community for over 51 years, offering a stage for original music and the diverse, underground sounds that define our city’s spirit.

Intimate Space

Our intimate space gives you an up close and revealing look at some of the best local music. Stand at the bar and take it all in or grab a booth in the restaurant area and enjoy it from a distance.

More like seeing your favorite group in your living room, rather than a music club, the Goose transports you to old England where the local pub is a traditional place for a pint, a chat, and some lively entertainment.

Live Music

From indie pop to Americana, acoustic folk to reggae, bluegrass to rockabilly, rock & roll to jazz, Fox & Goose showcases original performances by both local talent and nationally touring up-and-coming bands and songwriters, all contributing to the rich tapestry of Sacramento’s creative scene.
