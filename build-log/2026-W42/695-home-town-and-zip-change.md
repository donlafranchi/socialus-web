# F103.5 · #695, F081.4 · #568: the home town, and the zip changes on /you

- `members.home_market_key`, `home_town`, `home_town_geoid`, and `zip_place` (126 zips of the Sacramento market's four counties → Census Place), generated by `scripts/generate-home-town-migration.ts` from the Census 2020 ZCTA relationship files. `zip_home(zip)` resolves the market and, when it is open, the home town (a Place, else "county remainder"). `backfill_home_towns(market)` gives a waiting market's members theirs the day it opens: **#616's open step must call it.**
- `member.signup_profile.set` writes the market and home town in the same statement, resolved from the zip by the database. New `member.zip.set` (change the zip on /you): unknown zip refused with "We don't recognize that zip, try again.", otherwise zip, market, home town and home metro follow. `/you` has a Zip row ("Change zip"); the browser is told the area, never the town.
- The home town is private: members are readable by their owner only, and a guard test lists the only files that may name it.

Judgement call, flagged: "the Place the zip mostly lies in" by land area calls most zips a county remainder (Davis's zip is 76% rural), so a Place counts when it holds at least a tenth of the zip's land and the largest such Place wins. A population weighting would be better; the HUD swap is the moment to do it.
