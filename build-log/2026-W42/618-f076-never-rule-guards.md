# F076 · #618: standing guards for the never-rules (criteria 2, 3, 12, 14)

`tests/f076-never-rules.test.ts` scans for what must not appear: a metro looked up by IP, device position or distance and no pre-checked market or role on the waitlist surfaces (2); a role, creator or patron column on any member table, or the waitlist role read outside the two join handlers (3); any write to `is_open` in `src`, `scripts` or a migration other than the two that seed the launch metro and the markets (12); and any reader of the live counters other than the join handlers and the cache (14). Criterion 14 also runs the handler for real: one new address, the same address again and another known one return the same full response, and two rows are written. Each guard was seen failing against a mutant (a visitor-IP read, a pre-checked radio, an `is_open` write in a handler or a migration, a member-table role column, a handler that returns the live count, an action that selects the counters). The signed-in popup action no longer selects the counters at all (its select had carried them since before #614).

Criterion 16 was struck on 2026-10-11 (no list of markets to order); its surviving rule, no live count on any surface, is the criterion 14 scan. No product change.
