### change #222 (F081) + #489 — the signup screen and the three legal pages

**Signup (F081).** `OnboardingFlow` is now the one screen: legal name, display name, zip, and "I'm 18 or older and agree to the Terms" (Terms linked beside it). The email is the login. Every sign-in method (password, magic link, Google) passes through it. `completeOnboardingAction` validates (`src/lib/signup/profile.ts`), looks the zip up once to one metro (`src/lib/signup/zip-metro.ts`: crosswalk MSA → metro; unknown zip → no metro, never a default or nearest), and writes through the new handler `member.signup_profile.set` (legal name, display name, zip, `home_metro_id`, `adult_confirmed_at`). The next screen shows which metro; nobody picks one. A zip with no metro goes on to the existing waitlist question. **Migration** `20261007180000_member_signup_fields.sql` adds `legal_name`, `zip`, `adult_confirmed_at` (members are readable by themselves only).
**Not built here:** the national HUD-USPS crosswalk (only Sacramento's 126 zips are loaded, so "an unknown zip is refused" can't hold yet: such a zip is accepted and sent to the waitlist step); changing the zip on /you; phone verification (still behind `PHONE_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`).
**Legal pages (#489).** Terms and Privacy are drafts written only from rulings; `counsel` lists what a lawyer must supply (never rendered); a draft note shows on both. The three links are also on the first signup screen (the footer is hidden on phones and on auth screens).
