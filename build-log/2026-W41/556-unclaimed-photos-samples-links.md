### change #556 — every unclaimed Page ships with pictures, labelled sample posts and checked links

Don, 2026-10-10 (top priority; the photo hold is lifted for good, do not ask again): the 85 live unclaimed Pages and the first 26 listings had no photos.

- **Pictures.** `src/lib/unclaimed/photos.ts` reads the business's own homepage (`site-scrape.ts`: og:image, twitter:image, JSON-LD, on-page photos, widest srcset), keeps real photographs (size, aspect, tonal range, no flat promo cards, no near-duplicates) and credits them to the business. Behind them, free-license stock that matches the category: `scripts/unclaimed/stock.json`, 20 hand-picked Unsplash photos for each of nine pools (looked at on contact sheets, brand names and logos left out). Every picture is downloaded, re-encoded to WebP (the bucket takes only that; it strips EXIF) and uploaded to `media` under the system member, so a Page never hot-links. Each Page gets four: the cover (`photo_url`, with `photo_credit` and `photo_source_url`) and three that ride on its sample posts. There is no gallery feature (ruled 2026-10-05: one credited picture per Page, a gallery after beta), so the sample posts' pictures are the gallery. A stock cover says so: "Unsplash (stock photo, not this business)". `photo-overrides.json` lists businesses whose own pictures are posters, so a stock photo leads.
- **Sample posts.** Three per Page (an event, a deal, a last-minute opening), each with a picture, starting "Sample ·" and ending "This is a sample post … It is not a real offer", with `how_to_find`. `page_posts.sample_kind` (migration `20261010010000`) marks them; the loader rolls a stale one to the next day each run. `src/lib/unclaimed/samples.ts`.
- **Links.** `links.ts` checks every website and social link before anything is written (404/410, a server error that stays, a bad certificate, no answer = broken; a site that turns robots away is alive) and, after, that every Page opens on the live site and every picture loads. A broken website, a Page without its pictures, or one that does not open fails the run. Two were broken and are fixed in `link-fixes.json`: Smith Gallery (needs `www.`) and Fog Willow Farms (its certificate is wrong; the Page now cites its Facebook page, found on its own site). Socials come from the listing or the business's own site; a broken one is dropped.
- **Loader and builder.** `scripts/unclaimed/load.ts` now creates what is missing, then enriches every Page that lacks a cover or sample posts (idempotent), for the maker lists and the 26 listings (`listings.json`, new `planListings`; a listing whose site is already a maker row is one Page). A new business added to either list ships with all of it or the run fails. Phones come from the site where it gives one (`contact_phone`), logged in `page_sources`.
- **Not done:** hours, and a real gallery on the Page.

Tests: site-scrape, photos (synthetic pictures), samples, links, plan (every Page has a stock pool), and the loader against Postgres (enrichment once, stale roll-forward, too few pictures writes nothing, address fix).
