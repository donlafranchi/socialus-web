### change #463 — "Unfinished Pages" on You, with Continue and Delete

The drafts a member started in Create used to sit in "Your Pages" next to live ones with a small tag, and could not be removed (the PM, 2026-10-07: owners must be able to delete unpublished drafts). They now have their own "Unfinished Pages" block under Your Pages, each with Continue (back to the draft at its address) and Delete (a confirm sheet like a post's, then gone). `group.discard_draft` deletes a draft and what hangs off it at once, the way `purge_deleted_pages()` removes a Page: a draft reached nobody, so there is no 14-day grace and nothing to restore. Managing role only, and only while it is a draft; a live or archived Page still goes through `group.delete`. Posts have no draft state of their own, so there is nothing to discard there. No migration.
