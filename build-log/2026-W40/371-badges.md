# change #371 — badges, launch scope

The kind facts (Family-owned, Since, Co-op, Nonprofit, Free to join, Everyone welcome) are stored in groups.metadata.badges and set from a Badges sheet in Settings. Locally owned comes from the registration, for businesses only, and reads "Locally owned". Up to three navy chips with a gold mark sit under the kind line, the rest behind "+N more". Each opens what it means and "The owner says this. SocialUs hasn't checked it." (or the registration line). They show signed in only. No migration. The card badge waits on Explore's feed carrying metadata.
